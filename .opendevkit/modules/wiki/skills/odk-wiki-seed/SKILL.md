---
name: odk-wiki-seed
description: Seed or refresh the ODK Wiki from screened repository and Git history sources
---

This command enters through the ODK orchestrator. Read `.opendevkit/modules/wiki/workflows/wiki-seed.md` and delegate the entire seed operation to one Wiki specialist. Do not scan or seed unless the user explicitly invokes this command or explicitly opts in through Readiness. The Wiki specialist returns results, exclusions, errors, or clarification needs to ODK.
