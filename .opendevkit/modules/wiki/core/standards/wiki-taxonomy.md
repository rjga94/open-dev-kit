# Adaptive Wiki taxonomy

The Wiki is one OKF bundle rooted at `.opendevkit/wiki/`. Organize concept pages with directories and navigation in that bundle; do not create nested or separate OKF bundles for categories.

Suggested areas such as overview, architecture, development, operations, decisions, and reference are examples only. Create a category only when eligible project knowledge gives it useful content. Do not create empty directories or placeholder categories to match a starter tree.

During whole-Wiki maintenance, keep categories that still help readers find distinct topics. Add a project-specific category when a coherent body of knowledge warrants it. Merge, split, move, rename, or remove categories when that improves clarity, preserving unique knowledge and provenance and updating affected links and `index.md` navigation. Leave unaffected pages and categories unchanged.
